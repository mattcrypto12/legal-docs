## 2. Description of the App

Cadence is a non-custodial tool for scheduling recurring Solana payments ("standing orders"). Depending on the version of the App and your settings, features may include:

- saving payees (public addresses with labels);
- creating recurring payments in SOL or supported SPL tokens on a chosen schedule;
- preparing each due payment and submitting it to the Solana network after you approve and sign it with your device's Seed Vault;
- reminders for due payments; and
- payment history and cluster/RPC selection.

The App is non-custodial: it holds no private keys, has no delegated spending authority, and cannot move funds without your per-payment Seed Vault signature. We may add, remove, change, suspend, or limit features at any time.
