## 2. Description of the App

SeedAuth is a two-factor authentication (2FA) utility that generates time-based and counter-based one-time passwords (TOTP/HOTP) on your device. Depending on the version of the App and your settings, features may include:

- adding accounts by scanning provisioning QR codes, importing Google Authenticator export QR codes, or manual entry;
- encrypted local storage of authenticator secrets protected by device secure hardware and biometrics;
- optional binding of the encrypted vault to the device's Seed Vault so that a backup can be unlocked on a device restored from the same seed phrase;
- user-initiated encrypted backup files protected by a seed-derived key and/or a password; and
- security settings such as auto-lock, clipboard clearing, and screenshot blocking.

We may add, remove, change, suspend, or limit features at any time.
