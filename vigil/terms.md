## 2. Description of the App

Vigil is a read-only monitoring utility for public Solana blockchain addresses. Depending on the version of the App and your settings, features may include:

- watching public addresses and displaying balances and parsed transaction activity;
- notifications about activity on watched addresses;
- heuristic detection of suspected address-poisoning attempts (lookalike addresses);
- a pre-send address checker comparing an address against your saved contacts, local history, and lookalike patterns; and
- a local trusted-contacts address book.

The App is non-custodial in the strongest sense: it contains no wallet, holds no keys, and cannot create, sign, or submit transactions. We may add, remove, change, suspend, or limit features at any time.
